# Presentations

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `SethuCMS-Technical-Blueprint.pptx` | The SethuCMS deck, 23 slides, editable in PowerPoint, LibreOffice Impress or Google Slides. Speaker notes are in each slide's notes |
| `SethuCMS-Technical-Blueprint.pdf` | The same deck as a PDF, for sharing |

## What is in the deck

- Slides 1 to 6: the problem, the market, why now (licence changes), competitors and where SethuCMS fits.
- Slides 7 to 17: the blueprint: gateway, request pipeline, adapters and what each was tested on, security, drafts and publishing, capabilities, the SDK, the live gateway, migration, status and roadmap.
- Slides 18 and 19: upcoming features and the planned Free, Pro and Ultra plans (planned, not built; see doc 27).
- Slides 20 to 22: business model, commitments and the ask.
- Slide 23: close.

## Rules for keeping it honest

- Every claim must match `docs/` and the verified list on slide 16. If an adapter or feature is not tested on a real server, the deck says so.
- Market figures are analyst estimates. Re-check competitor funding and star counts before quoting them.
- Slides 18 and 19 are future plans. Prices and limits are not set.
- The deck has no company names for customers or design partners, because there are none yet.

## Editing

Open the `.pptx` and edit the text directly. After a change, export the PDF again with File, Export as PDF. Fonts are Arial and Courier New so the file looks the same on any computer.
